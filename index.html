About this map
Northeast PA here means Bradford, Carbon, Columbia, Lackawanna, Luzerne, Monroe, Pike, Schuylkill, Sullivan, Susquehanna, Wayne and Wyoming counties, with Lehigh, Northampton, Northumberland and Montour available as nearby extras. Streams that share a name are told apart by location, but two same-named streams within a couple of miles of each other can be merged. Stream classes, stocking and special regulations: Pennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission. Public land: PA DCNR (State Forest, State Parks) and PA Game Commission (State Game Lands). Basemaps: USGS The National Map, OpenStreetMap. Species come from two PFBC sources: Class A wild trout surveys (brook or brown), and 2025 and 2026 stocking records (brook, brown, rainbow, golden). Wild trout streams that are neither Class A nor stocked have no species in any statewide PFBC layer, so they show Not reported. Public land share is calculated on this map from the land boundaries shown (open public land only), so it matches the green sections. It does not use PFBC's own percentage. Land boundaries are simplified for display. Public / private access: a stream section is marked open public land when it falls inside a State Game Land, State Forest, State Park, PFBC property, or a parcel in the PA Conserved Lands layer (PALTA / WeConservePA, via DCNR) whose Access is Open. That layer covers federal, county and municipal land and land-trust easements. Parcels listed Restricted or Unknown are shown in yellow. Everything else has no public-land record and is likely private, but that is not a legal determination. Easements that allow angling on private land are not fully captured. Open land is not always open to fishing, so check posting and permissions. Stocking schedule: the PFBC trout stocking schedule (fbweb.pa.gov/TroutStocking), captured Nov 20, 2026 for the 2026 season (preseason, in-season and fall stockings, with species, meeting place, time and hatchery). It is a saved snapshot, not live, and is matched to streams by name and county, so a few streams with unusual names may show none. Dates and places can change; PFBC posts the next season's schedule in winter, and this page must be updated to include it. Verify access, posted land and regulations before you fish.
